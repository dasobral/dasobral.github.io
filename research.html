Ph.D. thesis

The Role of Relativistic Effects in Future Cosmic Surveys

University of Geneva, 2024 · Advisor: Camille Bonvin · Très bien

The thesis asked a narrow question: can galaxy surveys measure how gravity distorts time, separately from space? Light from every galaxy loses a little energy climbing out of gravitational wells, which slightly shifts where a survey places it. Isolating that shift gives a test of general relativity that doesn’t assume dark matter falls the way we think it does.[8][9] Surveys such as the SKA should measure it to 10–30%.
