01 / Profile

From cosmology
to secure systems.

I started in fundamental physics, completed a Ph.D. in theoretical cosmology at the University of Geneva, and moved into engineering through quantum-safe communications.

Today at Quside, I build integration workflows around QRNG entropy, cryptographic applications, CUDA, TLS/mTLS, and containerized deployments. Earlier, I worked on GEO-QKD control software and hybrid QKD/PQC integrations.
